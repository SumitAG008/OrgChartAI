## 📋 STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS

### Step 1: Click "HRIS Connections" in Left Sidebar
- Look at the **LEFT SIDEBAR** (left side of screen)
- Find **"HRIS Connections"** (with building icon 🔵)
- **Click it**

### Step 2: Click "Add Connection" Button
- You'll see a page titled "HRIS Connections"
- At the top right, there's a **GREEN button** that says **"Add Connection"**
- **Click it**

### Step 3: Fill in SuccessFactors Details
A modal opens. Fill in:

```
Connection Name: My SuccessFactors Connection
System: SAP SuccessFactors
Authentication Method: Basic Auth

Company ID: [Your Company ID]
Username: [Your Username]
Password: [Your Password]
API URL: https://api.successfactors.eu
```

### Step 4: Test Connection
- Click **"Test Connection"** button
- Wait for ✅ "Connection successful"
- Click **"Save"**

### Step 5: Auto Sync (ONE CLICK!)
After saving, you'll see your connection card with a **PURPLE "Auto Sync" button** (⚡ icon)

**Click "Auto Sync"** → Enter credentials → Click "Start Auto Sync"

**DONE!** Backend automatically:
- ✅ Discovers all entities
- ✅ Creates mappings
- ✅ Syncs all data
